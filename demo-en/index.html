mama-fufood

Log your baby's first foods: what they ate each time, how much, whether there was an allergic reaction, and whether they liked it, all organized into a calendar, statistics and simple results.

Your data stays with you

  • Your baby's name, records and photos are stored only on your phone, or in your own Google Drive when you turn on cloud features.
  • Export files are fully encrypted and can only be imported with the Google account that created them. We never have the key.
  • No ads and no analytics tools.

The App is for record-keeping and reference only. Its results are not medical judgements. If your baby has any reaction, please consult a doctor.
