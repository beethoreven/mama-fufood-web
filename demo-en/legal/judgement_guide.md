## What this page is about

The icons on the Food Results page and when adding a record are results the App produces from the records you enter, using fixed rules. This page publishes those rules in full.

> **The App is for record-keeping and reference only. Its results are not medical judgements.**
>
> - The rules below are our own way of organizing records. **They were not derived from medical knowledge or medical research and have no medical basis**, and there is no recognized standard to follow.
> - The results only arrange your records in an easier-to-read form. **They are not medical judgements and cannot be used to determine whether your baby is allergic.**
> - The App is meant to help you keep records. If your baby has any reaction, please consult a doctor.

The rules are built into the App. When they change, the change takes effect through an App update and is explained on this page.

## Allergy status

### How it is calculated

Only records with an "allergy status" filled in are counted; records without a selection are left out. Each record is scored by the option you chose:

| You chose | Score |
|---|---|
| No allergic reaction | 0 |
| Allergic reaction the next day | 0.3 |
| Allergic reaction the same day | 1 |

All scores for the food are added up and divided by the number of filled-in records to get the average score:

(≤ means "less than or equal to"; < means "less than".)

| Average score | Shown as |
|---|---|
| 0 ≤ average ≤ 0.2 | Stable |
| 0.2 < average ≤ 0.7 | Risk |
| 0.7 < average ≤ 1 | Allergy |

### Additional minimum labels

So that the average does not water down important events, the result is at least the following when:

- "Same day" has appeared once → at least "Risk"
- "Same day" has appeared 2 or more times → "Allergy"
- "Next day" has appeared 2 or more times → at least "Risk" (with only 1 "next day", the average score applies)

### When no icon is shown

If there are fewer than 3 filled-in records and "same day" or "next day" has never appeared, there are not enough records yet and **no icon is shown**. As soon as "same day" or "next day" has appeared once, the rules above apply.

### Examples

| Records for this food | Calculation | Shown as |
|---|---|---|
| None, none | Only 2 records, no reaction so far | No icon |
| None, none, none | Average 0 | Stable |
| None, none, none, next day | Average 0.3 ÷ 4 ≈ 0.08; only 1 next day | Stable |
| None, same day | Average 0.5; same day has appeared | Risk |
| None, none, none, same day, same day | Average 0.4; same day twice | Allergy |

### Please remember

- **"Stable" only means that no reaction appears in the records so far. It does not mean the food is safe.** An allergy may appear only after several exposures, or on the very first one.
- **When your baby eats several foods on the same day, the App cannot know which one caused a reaction.** Please mark records using your own judgement; the results depend on how you mark them.
- "Allergy" means allergic reactions have appeared several times; we suggest consulting a doctor.

## Eating response (preference)

### How it is calculated

Only records with an "eating response" filled in are counted. Each record is scored by the option you chose:

| You chose | Score |
|---|---|
| Dislike | -1 |
| Accept | 0 |
| Like | 1 |

A baby's taste changes over repeated tries, so only recent records are used: the App compares "the last 10 records" with "all records from the last 3 months", takes the group with **more records**, and calculates the average score:

| Average score | Shown as |
|---|---|
| -1 ≤ average < -0.2 | Dislike |
| -0.2 ≤ average < 0.2 | Accept |
| 0.2 ≤ average ≤ 1 | Like |

### When no icon is shown

If the chosen group has fewer than 3 records, there are not enough records yet and **no icon is shown**. Your baby might have disliked a food twice, or liked it twice; with so few tries, we do not draw a conclusion.

### Examples

| Records used | Calculation | Shown as |
|---|---|---|
| Like 6 times, accept 3 times, dislike once | (6 - 1) ÷ 10 = 0.5 | Like |
| Dislike twice, accept once | -2 ÷ 3 ≈ -0.67 | Dislike |
| Dislike, like | Only 2 records | No icon |

### Why only recent records are used

A baby's taste may change over repeated tries, and a food that was disliked at first may gradually be accepted. So early records do not decide the result forever.

## Diarrhea and vomiting

On the "Diarrhea & Vomiting" tab, each food has two results, one for diarrhea and one for vomiting, calculated separately. A food might often come with diarrhea but never with vomiting.

### How it is calculated

Only records with "diarrhea or vomiting" filled in are counted; records where none of the three options is selected are left out. Diarrhea and vomiting are scored separately: 1 point if it happened, 0 if not; choosing "Normal" gives 0 for both. The scores are added up and divided by the number of filled-in records to get the average score:

(≤ means "less than or equal to"; < means "less than".)

| Average score | Shown as |
|---|---|
| 0 ≤ average ≤ 0.4 | Normal |
| 0.4 < average ≤ 1 | Diarrhea (the vomiting column shows "Vomiting") |

Only the average score is used; there is no "at least Risk after a certain number of times" rule like the one for allergies.

### When no icon is shown

If there are fewer than 3 filled-in records and diarrhea (or vomiting) has never happened, there are not enough records yet and **no icon is shown**. As soon as it has happened once, the average score applies.

### Examples

| Filled-in records | Diarrhea | Vomiting |
|---|---|---|
| Normal, normal, normal, diarrhea, diarrhea | 2 ÷ 5 = 0.4 → Normal | 0 → Normal |
| Normal, diarrhea, diarrhea + vomiting | 2 ÷ 3 ≈ 0.67 → Diarrhea | 1 ÷ 3 ≈ 0.33 → Normal |
| Diarrhea | 1 ÷ 1 = 1 → Diarrhea | Only 1 record, never happened → No icon |

### Please remember

- **"Normal" only means that it mostly has not happened in the records so far. It does not mean the food cannot cause diarrhea or vomiting.**
- **When your baby eats several foods on the same day, the App cannot know which one caused it.** Please record using your own judgement; the results depend on how you record things.
- When your baby has diarrhea or vomits, watch for signs of dehydration (for example, less urine or lower alertness) and consult a doctor.

## What no icon means

For allergy status, eating response, and diarrhea and vomiting alike, **no icon means there are not enough records yet for the App to give a result**. Foods that have never been recorded show no icon either.
