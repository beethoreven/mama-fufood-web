## 1. What the App is

1. The App is a tool for logging a baby's first foods. It helps you record what your baby ate each time, how much, whether there was an allergic reaction and how your baby responded, and organizes those records into statistics and simple results.
2. The App is sold as a one-time purchase on Google Play and is available only in Taiwan. Purchases and payments are handled by Google Play. (Well, that was the original plan, but things have not gone our way so far.)
3. You can log records on your phone without signing in. Updating shared data such as the food list, cloud sync, export and import, and sending feedback require signing in with a Google account.

## 2. The App is not a medical service

1. **The App is for record-keeping and reference only. Its results are not medical judgements, and it does not provide any diagnosis, treatment or medical advice.** The labels "Stable", "Risk", "Allergy", "Dislike", "Accept" and "Like" shown in the App are produced by fixed rules from the records you enter. They have no medical basis and do not represent a medical judgement.
2. "Stable" only means that no reaction appears in the records so far. **It does not mean the food is safe for your baby.** An allergy may appear only after several exposures, or on the very first one.
3. When your baby eats several foods on the same day, the App cannot tell which one caused a reaction, and the results depend on how you record things.
4. If your baby shows any discomfort or a possible allergic reaction, please consult a doctor. In a serious situation, such as difficulty breathing, swelling of the lips or face, or a marked drop in alertness, seek medical care immediately or call 119.
5. The rules are our own way of organizing records. They were not derived from medical knowledge or medical research, and there is no recognized standard to follow. The full rules are published on the App's "How Results Are Determined" page. The rules are built into the App, and changes are made through App updates (see Section 6).

## 3. Where your data is stored and who looks after it

1. Your baby's name and records are stored only on your phone. When you turn on "Sync records to cloud", they are also stored in your own Google Drive. Our server does not keep this data (for the only exception, see Section 4, item 4).
2. Because the data is not in our hands, **you are responsible for keeping and backing it up**. We recommend turning on "Sync records to cloud" and "Save photos to cloud" and exporting regularly. If your phone is lost or damaged, or the App is uninstalled, and you have neither turned on cloud features nor exported, your data may be impossible to recover.
3. Your phone's operating system automatically backs up some App data to your own cloud account: Android backs up to your Google account, and iPhone backs up to your iCloud when iCloud Backup is on. This is a feature of the operating system, does not go through us, and is not guaranteed to restore the latest data. Photos are not included in automatic backups.
4. When you log the same baby on several phones, **please sign in with the same Google account on every phone**. Using different accounts, or editing on several phones while offline, may leave the data inconsistent. In such cases the App offers only basic handling (for example, letting you choose which copy to keep) and cannot guarantee a complete merge.
5. Shared data (for example, the food list and units) is updated separately on each phone. If phones update at different times, records made on another phone that this phone does not recognize yet are hidden for now and appear once this phone updates its shared data. The data itself is not lost.
6. The App supports 10 records per baby per day. If sync or import brings a day above 10 records, the data is kept, but no more records can be added for that day. This usually happens when several phones log at the same time, and the App only guarantees basic usability in that case.
7. Each phone can hold up to 8 babies.

## 4. Export and import

1. Export files are saved to your own Google Drive and are **fully encrypted (photos included)**. The encryption key is stored in an area of your Google Drive that only the App can read, so a file can only be imported while signed in with the same Google account.
2. **We do not have the key and cannot decrypt export files for you.** If you can no longer sign in to the Google account used for the export, or you delete the App's data in Google Drive, the export file can no longer be opened, and no one, including us, can recover it. Export files from one Google account cannot be imported under a different account.
3. An older version of the App cannot import files exported by a newer version. Please update the App first.
4. When you import a file exported by an older version of the App and its data format is not compatible, the App temporarily sends the record data in the file (photos excluded) to our server to convert the format. **We do not keep this data, either before or after conversion.** Each account can use this conversion up to 5 times per day.
5. Before importing, the App turns off "Sync records to cloud" and "Save photos to cloud". After the import, please turn them back on yourself; the App then reconciles differences with the cloud data using the first-sync process.
6. If a baby with the same name already exists on your phone, you can choose to keep the data on the phone or replace it with the imported data. A baby whose name has changed is treated as a different baby. If the import would bring the total number of babies above 8, it cannot be imported.
